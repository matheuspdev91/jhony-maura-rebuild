# JHONY & MAURA — VISUAL RULES

## Direction

The landing page must feel:

- premium
- cinematic
- editorial
- sophisticated
- elegant
- intimate
- intentional

The visual quality must come primarily from composition, typography, imagery, spacing and restrained motion.

## Cinematic Motion

Use a restrained combination of:
- real, perceptible parallax
- depth layers
- image reveals
- subtle scale
- opacity
- typography movement
- overlapping compositions
- clip-path where useful
- section transitions
- refined hover micro-interactions

Parallax must be perceptible. Do not reduce it to an almost invisible 10–20px movement.

Do not animate everything.

The page must still look premium with motion disabled.

## Hero

The existing hero media/video is the visual protagonist.

Use cinematic composition, depth and restrained choreography without covering the media with unnecessary UI.

## Music Marquee

The music genres are TWO CONTINUOUS TYPOGRAPHIC TRACKS.

Track 1 moves continuously in one direction.
Track 2 moves continuously in the opposite direction.

They are visual/editorial motion bands, NOT cards, NOT a carousel and NOT static lists.

Prefer CSS animation.

The loop must be seamless and lightweight.

## Experience

Do NOT use a large scroll-driven narrative system.

Use a normal page section with a lightweight cinematic autoplay carousel when multiple experience images are available.

Native page scrolling must remain natural.

No scroll-jacking.
